F1 history

Formula One originated from the World Manufacturers' Championship (1925–1930) and European Drivers' Championship (1931–1939).
The formula is a set of rules that all participants' cars must follow.
before World War II, several Grand Prix racing organisations made suggestions for a new championship to replace the European Championship,
but due to the suspension of racing during the conflict, a new International Formula for cars did not become formalised until after the war.

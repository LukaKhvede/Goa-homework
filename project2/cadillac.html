Cadillac F1 Team

About Cadillac F1

Cadillac F1 Team is the newest team in Formula 1 joining the grid in 2026 as the 11th team. The American team is backed by General Motors under the Cadillac brand making them the first American manufacturer to enter Formula 1 since the early days of the sport. The team is based in the United States and represents a huge moment for American motorsport. They use Ferrari power units while developing their own engine for the future.

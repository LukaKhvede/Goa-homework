Alpine A526

About the A526

The Alpine A526 is the team's car for the 2026 season built around Renault's new power unit developed for the 2026 regulations. Alpine is one of only four teams to design and build their own engine in house. The 2026 regulations with their increased electrical power deployment are seen as an opportunity for Alpine to close the gap to the front runners.

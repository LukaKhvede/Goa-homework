About the Audi R26

The Audi R26 is the first Formula 1 car developed by Audi for their entry into the sport. Built with German engineering precision, the car represents Audi’s ambition to compete at the highest level of motorsport. It is expected to use a hybrid power unit while focusing heavily on aerodynamics and efficiency.

About Red Bull Racing

Red Bull Racing is an Austrian Formula 1 team founded in 2005. The team is based in Milton Keynes, England. Red Bull has won 6 Constructors Championships and their drivers have won 7 Drivers Championships. Max Verstappen won four consecutive titles from 2021 to 2024 making him one of the greatest champions in the sport. The team is known for their innovative car designs and strong pit stop performance.

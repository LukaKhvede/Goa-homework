About Haas

Haas F1 Team is an American Formula 1 team that entered the sport in 2016. Founded by Gene Haas the team is based in Kannapolis, North Carolina with their racing operations in Banbury, England. Haas is unique in that they source many components from Ferrari under the rules allowing customer teams. They use Ferrari power units and have a technical partnership with the Italian team. Haas had their best season in 2018 finishing fifth in the Constructors Championship.

Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1

About Mercedes

Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula One Team is a German-British team based in Brackley, England. The team dominated Formula 1 from 2014 to 2021, winning 8 consecutive Constructors Championships. Lewis Hamilton won 6 of his 7 world titles with the team. Mercedes is powered by their own engine built in Brixworth and are considered one of the most technically advanced teams in the paddock.

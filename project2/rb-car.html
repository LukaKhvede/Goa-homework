About the VCARB 02

The RB VCARB 02 is the team's 2026 challenger. Like their sister team Red Bull Racing, RB uses the Ford Red Bull Powertrains engine for 2026. The car is designed at their Faenza base and shares some design philosophy with the main Red Bull car while having its own unique characteristics to suit the driving styles of their lineup.

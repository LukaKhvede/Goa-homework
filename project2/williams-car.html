About the FW48

The Williams FW48 is the team's 2026 challenger powered by Mercedes engines. Williams have been on an upward trajectory and with Carlos Sainz joining the team they hope to make a significant step forward. The new 2026 regulations give all teams a chance to reset and Williams with their improving infrastructure are well placed to take advantage.

About McLaren

McLaren Racing is a British Formula 1 team founded by Bruce McLaren in 1963. Based in Woking, England, McLaren has won 8 Constructors Championships and 12 Drivers Championships. The team had legendary drivers like Ayrton Senna, Alain Prost, and Mika Hakkinen. After a difficult period, McLaren made a strong comeback in 2023 and 2024 winning the Constructors Championship in 2024 with Lando Norris challenging strongly for the drivers title.

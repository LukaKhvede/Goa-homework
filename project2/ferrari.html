Scuderia Ferrari

About Ferrari

Scuderia Ferrari is the oldest and most successful team in Formula 1 history. Founded in 1929 by Enzo Ferrari, the team has been competing in Formula 1 since the very first World Championship season in 1950. Ferrari has won 16 Constructors Championships and 15 Drivers Championships. The team is based in Maranello, Italy and is known worldwide by their iconic red color, called Rosso Corsa.

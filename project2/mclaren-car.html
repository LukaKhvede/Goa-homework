About the MCL40

The McLaren MCL40 is the team's 2026 contender as they look to defend their Constructors Championship title. McLaren uses Mercedes power units and their Woking factory has been significantly upgraded in recent years. The MCL40 follows the all new 2026 regulations and McLaren hopes their strong development pace from 2023 and 2024 will carry over into the new era.

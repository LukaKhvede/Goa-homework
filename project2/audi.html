About Audi F1

Audi F1 Team is entering Formula 1 as part of their long-term motorsport project. The German manufacturer is preparing to join the grid with full factory support and strong engineering background from Audi Sport. Their entry represents a major step for Audi in top-level motorsport, aiming to compete with top teams like Ferrari, Mercedes, and Red Bull.

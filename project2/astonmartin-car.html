About the AMR26

The Aston Martin AMR26 marks a new chapter for the team as they switch to Honda power units for 2026. The Honda engine is expected to be one of the strongest on the grid giving Aston Martin a significant competitive boost. The team's new factory and wind tunnel are now fully operational giving them excellent development resources.

About Aston Martin

Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team is a British team based in Silverstone, England. The team rebranded from Racing Point to Aston Martin in 2021. With significant investment from Lawrence Stroll they built a brand new state of the art factory at Silverstone. The team signed Fernando Alonso in 2023 and he delivered some brilliant performances. For 2026 the team switches to Honda power units which is seen as a major boost.

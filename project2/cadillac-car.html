Cadillac F1 01

About the Cadillac F1 01

The Cadillac F1 01 is the team's debut car in Formula 1. As a brand new team entering under the 2026 regulations they have designed the car from scratch at their American base. Using Ferrari power units gives them a competitive engine while they develop their own GM power unit for future seasons. The car is a major milestone for American motorsport and General Motors.

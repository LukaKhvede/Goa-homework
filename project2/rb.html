About RB F1 Team

RB F1 Team formerly known as AlphaTauri and before that Toro Rosso is Red Bull's junior team in Formula 1. Based in Faenza, Italy the team serves as a proving ground for young Red Bull junior drivers. The team has produced several top drivers including Sebastian Vettel, Max Verstappen, and Carlos Sainz. In 2024 the team rebranded to RB following a new sponsorship deal. They use the same Honda power units as Red Bull Racing.

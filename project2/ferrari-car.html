Ferrari SF-26

About the SF-26

The Ferrari SF-26 is Scuderia Ferrari's challenger for the 2026 Formula 1 World Championship. Built to comply with the all-new 2026 technical regulations, the car features a completely redesigned aerodynamic package and Ferrari's new V6 hybrid power unit. The SF-26 represents Ferrari's biggest technical reset in years as the team aims to challenge for both championships.

Red Bull RB22

About the RB22

The Red Bull RB22 is the team's 2026 challenger built under the new technical regulations. Red Bull partnered with Ford for their new power unit after Honda stepped back from full factory involvement. The car continues Red Bull's philosophy of low drag and exceptional downforce balance that made them so dominant in recent years.

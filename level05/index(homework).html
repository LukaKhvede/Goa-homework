Goa Martial Arts teaches you how to defend yourself using martial arts techniques.
Knowing martial arts is important because, first of all, it helps you in different situations and provides good physical activity.

Training Programs

STRIKING:
Striking involves using punches, kicks, elbows, and knees to hit your opponent.
It helps improve speed, power, and coordination.


GRAPLING:
Grappling focuses on controlling your opponent through holds, locks, and throws.
It teaches balance, strength, and self-defence without relying on punches or kicks.

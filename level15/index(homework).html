About ufc:

The Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) is an American mixed martial arts (MMA) promotion company based in Las Vegas, Nevada.
It is owned and operated by TKO Group Holdings, itself a majority owned subsidiary of Endeavor Group Holdings.
The largest MMA promotion in the world,the UFC has over 578 fighters contracted that fight across 11 weight divisions (eight men's and three women's)

Pound for Pund:

The Ultimate Fighting Championship introduced pound-for-pound (P4P) rankings to compare fighters from different weight classes based on skill rather than size.
As MMA grew in popularity in the early 2000s, fans and analysts wanted a way to determine who the best overall fighter was, regardless of weight.
The idea became widely recognized during the dominance of fighters like Anderson Silva and Georges St-Pierre, who were considered among the most complete athletes in the sport.
Over time, the UFC formalized P4P rankings through media voting, making it an official part of the promotion.Today, pound-for-pound rankings remain an important and debated part of MMA, highlighting the most skilled and accomplished fighters in the world across all divisions.

UFC CEO:

Dana Frederick White Jr is an American businessman who is the CEO and president of the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC), a global mixed martial arts organization.
In 2025, White's net worth was estimated at over $600 million by Forbes.
Outside the UFC, he is the owner of Power Slap, a slap fighting promotion he founded in 2022.

UFC announcer

Bruce Anthony Buffer is an American professional mixed martial arts ring announcer and the official octagon announcer for the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) events
introduced on broadcasts as the "Veteran Voice of the Octagon". Buffer's catchphrase is "It's time!"
which he announces before the main event of a UFC card.
